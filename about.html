About us

Care built on dignity, continuity, and trust.

BijonSikha exists to make elder care feel structured, compassionate, and reassuring for families who need dependable support in Kolkata.

Our model combines practical home support with the discipline of healthcare coordination, helping seniors live with greater ease while relatives gain more clarity and confidence, in collaboration with Manipal Hospitals.

Our story

Built from healthcare experience and family realities.

Origin

From a care gap families know too well

Families often struggle to coordinate caregiving, medical logistics, and emergency support at the same time. BijonSikha was shaped around solving exactly that.

Niramay group

Backed by a long-standing healthcare ecosystem

As part of the Niramay Group, BijonSikha grows from a legacy of practical care, operational trust, and community-focused service.

Medical partnership

Designed to stay close to clinical support

The approach is built in collaboration with Manipal Hospitals to complement hospital-grade medical pathways with home-based, family-facing care delivery.

Our mission

To help seniors age with dignity while giving families a more stable, transparent support system.

Our promise

We treat every family journey with seriousness, warmth, and consistent follow-through.

Our standard

Thoughtful communication, dependable scheduling, and care experiences that feel composed rather than chaotic.

Our focus

Keeping care personal, respectful, and practical across both everyday and high-pressure situations.
