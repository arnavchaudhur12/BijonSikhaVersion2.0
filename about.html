About BijonSikha

BijonSikha is a compassionate elder care service in Kolkata, dedicated to providing dignified and respectful care for seniors. We are backed by the medical excellence of Manipal Hospitals, ensuring access to top doctors and advanced technology.

Our Mission

Our mission is to enhance the quality of life for seniors by providing personalized care that respects their dignity and independence. We strive to create a supportive environment where seniors feel valued and cared for.

History of the Niramay Group

BijonSikha is a part of the Niramay Group, a trusted name in healthcare for over two decades. With a legacy of compassionate care and medical excellence, Niramay Group has been serving the community with dedication and integrity.

Collaboration with Manipal Hospitals

Our collaboration with Manipal Hospitals brings unparalleled medical expertise to our elder care services. This partnership ensures that our seniors have access to top doctors, advanced medical technology, and seamless healthcare support.
